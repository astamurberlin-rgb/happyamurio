## Specific rejected shortcuts for the remaining priorities

**Mastava / mashkhurda:** Novozhenov–Tityunnik1981 supplies500g output but its introduction explicitly says all ingredient weights are **gross**, not edible net. Therefore its lamb108g / beef109g cannot enter BLS edible-basis calculation unchanged. Need original preprocessing norms matched to meat cut/grade and vegetable season, or a directly net-weighted university card. Broth, fat/dairy identity and seasoning also remain unspecified. The catalog-page reproduction marking cleaned beef109g does not remove this source discrepancy. [Original book, introduction and p175](https://djvu.online/file/yk1RXcxMTD4ZZ).

**Dimlama:** Salikhov1991 variants have no finished gram yield; one vegetable formulation omits tomato/broth amounts although method mentions them. No assumed evaporation multiplier. A measured final edible batch or a stronger card is needed.

## Calculation guardrails

- Raw-net quantity × corresponding raw catalog value; independently apply only justified retention, once, then divide by final edible mass.
- Intermediate cooked meat/noodle masses are process checks, not duplicate ingredients and not universal retention factors.
- If choosing cooked catalog noodles instead, that is a different explicit calculation route; do not also count raw flour.
- Full soup broth retention does not mean vitamin retention100%. Fat skim/drain and greasing transfer need explicit treatment.
- Keep missing nutrients unknown; nutrient completeness is not source-recipe completeness.
